Enredo A motion tool: write a sentence, get an animation. (2026)

lab. Tool design · Front-end · Motion

read the brief

Enredo (“plot”) is a motion tool I designed and coded. You drop an image and write what happens; each verb becomes a scene on a pixel grid, in the order of the sentence. Tempo, grid, palette and format are adjustable, and it exports MP4, GIF or PNG frames. Personal project, 2026.

Uncanny Index A machine that reads a face, human or AI, through Peirce’s signs. (2026)

lab. Interface · Motion · Gen-AI · Poster

read the brief

Born from my design thesis at USP on images that feel almost human. The machine scans a portrait and diagnoses it through Peirce’s icon, index and symbol, measuring how deep it falls into the uncanny valley. Four animated screens and a closing poster, in beige and wine.
